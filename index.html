HOW TO PLAY

Keep your core covered.

Mouse / WASDMove. With WASD, aim separately using your mouse.Click / SPACEHold to launch pieces of your orbiting armor.Right click / EHold to pull loose scrap. You move slower while pulling.P / EscapePause your controls. The online arena keeps running; offline practice pauses completely.

Armor absorbs shots. A hit knocks a shield piece loose. Shots that slip through your orbit damage your core. Three core hits scrap you.

Pick up what others lose. Each new piece earns salvage points once per pilot; reclaiming the same piece rebuilds armor without farming points. Copper and gold scrap score more. Eliminations earn 75 points.

Repair at the cyan wells. Stay inside a + circle without firing. After combat settles, every three seconds restores one core segment.

Knocked out? You lose 20% of your score and return after 2.8 seconds with six armor pieces. Highest score after three minutes wins.

On touchscreens, drag to move and hold the Fire button to auto-aim at nearby rivals. Online rooms support eight human players, with bots filling the arena. Use Copy link to invite friends. Practice offline runs entirely in your browser.
