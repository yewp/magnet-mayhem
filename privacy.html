ORBBITZ.IO

Privacy policy

Last updated November 28, 2026. This policy covers the Orbbitz.io game on the web and its Android app, which shows the same web game.

Orbbitz.io is a small independent game. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your information. This page explains the little data the game does use.

Stored on your device

The game saves these in your browser's local storage so it remembers you between visits:

  • your pilot name, AI difficulty, chosen mode and graphics and sound settings;
  • your progress: experience level, personal best and the cosmetics you have claimed and equipped.

This stays on your device. You can delete it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it on Android. The game itself does not use cookies.

When you play online

Practice offline runs entirely on your device and sends nothing. When you use Play online or a private room, your device sends our game server:

  • your pilot name and the cosmetics on your ship;
  • your controls while you play (movement, aiming, firing, pulling) and the room code.

Other players in the same room see your pilot name, your ship and your score. Names with offensive words are shown as "Pilot". Please don't use your real name. Rooms live only in the server's memory while they are in use; they are not saved, and nothing about a match is kept after the room closes.

Services that run the game

The game server runs on Cloudflare, the website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Android app is distributed through Google Play. To deliver the game, these providers handle technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. We do not use that information to identify or track you.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and is not designed to collect it from anyone, including children. The only thing other players can see is the pilot name you choose.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/yewp/magnet-mayhem/issues.
